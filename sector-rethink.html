The companies aren't the point — sector health is. Each card drops the 4-name list and instead shows total m-cap, P/E, profit growth, today's return, a 30-day trend, and an advance/decline split. Answers "is this sector cheap, hot, or crowded?" before you click in. Genuinely new data, zero overlap with the m-cap lists elsewhere on the page.
